# Participant information: Replication Navigator pilot

*Template. Adapt it to your institution's ethics requirements before use.*

**What is this?** Replication Navigator is a tool that helps researchers plan replication studies. We are testing it at the OSM Summit, and we'd like to use data from this session in a methods paper.

**What would I do?** Use the tool in your team for about 90 minutes, then complete a short (about 4-minute) feedback survey.

**Is participation in the research voluntary?** Yes. When you start, you choose whether to tick the box that allows your usage to be logged. You can use the tool fully either way. You can change your choice in Settings at any time.

**What is logged if I agree?** A random ID created by your browser, messages you send to the AI coach and its replies, checklist ticks, ratings of replies, citations you open or flag, your survey answers, and your exported plan. We do **not** collect your name or email. Please don't type personal information into the tool.

**Who processes the data?** The app runs on Render (hosting). Messages to the coach are processed by Anthropic's API to generate replies. If you upload the paper you are replicating, it is sent to Anthropic's API once to extract key details and is not stored by us. Research logs are exported after the session and stored securely by the research team at [institution].

**How will it be used?** Only in aggregate or pseudonymised form, in research publications and presentations about the tool. [Retention period and open-data plans.]
