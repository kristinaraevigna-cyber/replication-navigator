# Expert rubric

## A. Replication-plan quality (score each exported plan)

Score each criterion **0 = absent / wrong, 1 = partly adequate, 2 = fully adequate**. Criteria follow the FORRT checklist and the Replication Recipe.

| # | criterion | stage | anchors for 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Target and specific claim are clearly identified and justified | 1 | Names one study and one claim; argues value **and** uncertainty; notes feasibility |
| 2 | Existing replications were searched for | 1 | Names the databases or searches used and the result |
| 3 | Replication type matches the stated aim | 2 | Type is explicit; purpose is stated; theory link is given |
| 4 | Replication goal is explicit and consistent later on | 2, 6, 9 | Goal chosen; sample size and analysis follow from it |
| 5 | Materials plan and author contact are appropriate | 3 | Inventory done; gaps and solutions documented; email professional |
| 6 | Reproduction was considered | 4 | Attempted, or explains why not possible |
| 7 | Measurement validity is addressed | 5 | Goes beyond alpha; validity checks planned; translation handled |
| 8 | Sample size is justified | 6 | Doesn't reuse the original N; accounts for inflation; approach fits the goal |
| 9 | Differences are documented and anticipated | 7 | Components rated; influential differences identified |
| 10 | Preregistration plan is concrete | 8 | Hypotheses, exclusions, analysis and contingencies specified |
| 11 | Success criteria are defined in advance and appropriate | 9 | Beyond significance; comparison method is sound |
| 12 | Interpretation plan is balanced | 10 | Non-accusatory; considers reasons for divergence; open sharing |

Total 0–24. Also record: the expert's overall judgement ("Would you approve this plan as a supervisor? yes / with revisions / no") and free comments.

## B. Coach-reply quality (sample of logged replies)

For each (reply, cited card) pair in `citation_audit_sample.csv`:

- **Support:** 2 = the card fully supports the claim it's attached to; 1 = partly or tangentially; 0 = doesn't support it or contradicts it.

For each reply as a whole:

- **Accuracy:** does it contain a methodological error? (y/n, and describe it)
- **Out-of-evidence claims:** substantive claims with no citation (count)
- **Helpfulness for a doctoral student:** 1–5
- **Tone:** appropriate / too directive / too vague

Rate blind to participant and team. Double-rate at least 20% and report κ.
