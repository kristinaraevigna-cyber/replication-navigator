    Welcome to Replication Navigator

    This tool walks you step by step through planning a replication. Each stage explains what to decide in plain language, gives you a short worksheet and checklist, and has an AI coach you can ask questions. The coach only uses a curated evidence base of nine key sources on replication, and cites where each piece of advice comes from.

    Time: about 1½–2 hours for stages 1–9 (less if some stages don't apply to you). At the end you download your plan and a draft preregistration. Your work is saved in this browser, so you can stop and come back on the same device.

    Research participation (optional)

    We are evaluating this tool for a methods paper. If you agree, we log your interactions with your pseudonymous code: coach messages, checklist ticks, ratings and survey answers. Your worksheet answers are logged only when you export your plan. You can use the tool fully without agreeing.
